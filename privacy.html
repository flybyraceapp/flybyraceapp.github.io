Privacy & advertising

Effective 26 July 2026

Career data

FlyByRace stores local preferences in your browser. When you sign in with Google, your career save and device connection status are stored in Firebase under your Google account UID.

Google services

The site uses Google Authentication and Firebase for account access, synchronized saves and communication with FlyByRace Link. Google may process account and technical data according to its own privacy policies.

Advertising

FlyByRace may display one manually placed Google AdSense banner at the bottom of career content. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize ads where permitted. Advertising is not loaded inside the FlyByRace Link desktop application.

Consent and choices

Where required, advertising consent is managed through a Google-certified consent platform. You can also control cookies through your browser and sign out of FlyByRace at any time.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the contact method published at flybyrace.com.
